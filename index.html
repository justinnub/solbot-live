How to read this page
Paper trading
Every trade here is simulated with virtual SOL, including realistic fees, slippage and delays. No wallet is connected and no real money moves.
Main bot
The default rule set. A test bot's rules replace it only after at least 30 closed trades show it makes money after fees.
Test bots
Variations trading the same coins side by side, each with its own paper account: different entry filters (how the coin launched, holder limits, buyer counts) and exits (stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop).
Real buyers
Different wallets buying at least 0.01 SOL in the last 5 minutes, not counting the coin's creator or wallets that bought in the launch block.
Safety checks
The dev cannot mint more or freeze tokens, the top 10 wallets do not hold too much, the dev has no history of dumping, and the market is a real PumpSwap pool.
Stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop
Sell when down a set amount; sell part when up enough to get the money back; let the rest ride and sell if it falls a set amount from its high.
Updates
The bot publishes new numbers every couple of minutes; this page checks every minute by itself.
